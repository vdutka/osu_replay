# Changelog

Published releases only. Internal development logs are not distributed.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-23

First public release.

### Added

- Profile score play buttons and an in-page osu!standard replay viewer.
- Stable/lazer replay decoding, supported mods and recorded-rate playback.
- Cursor, hit objects, hitsounds, judgement feedback, score/accuracy/combo HUD and timing-error display.
- Local WebAssembly PP with estimated reconstruction fallbacks and recorded/online completion values.
- Seeking, fullscreen, volume, dimming and 0.5×–3× playback speed.
- Local replay/map imports and clearable in-memory download caching.
- Ready-to-load extension, installation guide, privacy details and dependency notices.
